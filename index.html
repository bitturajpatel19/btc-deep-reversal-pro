CLOSED · No open trade
Open a paper trade or enable Auto Paper Mode to start.
Auto mode is OFF until enabled. Adjustment rule: one short leg must decay by at least 20% (current ask ≤ 80% of its entry premium) AND the opposite running leg's premium must rise by at least 7% above its entry premium (current ask ≥ 107% of entry). If the opposite leg's premium falls by 7% or more, this condition is NOT satisfied and no adjustment/liquidation happens because of this rule. When both conditions are met, book paper P&L on the decayed leg and select the replacement using the full original rules against current live BTC options spot: rebuild the PE/CE candidate pair within 5–9% OTM on each side, target ~7% OTM, and match their live sell-bid premiums as closely as possible; then reopen only the decayed side at its selected strike. The opposite running leg remains open. Overall TP/SL also remains active. No real orders are sent.
